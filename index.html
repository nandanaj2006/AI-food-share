Good food shouldn't end up in the bin while someone nearby goes hungry.

FoodShare connects restaurants, hotels, and stores that have surplus food with the NGOs, shelters, and volunteers who can collect and deliver it — matched and prioritised automatically, before it spoils.

How a donation moves

List it

A donor posts surplus food

Restaurants, hostels, and stores log what's left over, how much, and by when it needs to be collected.

Match it

The system prioritises requests

Nearby NGOs and volunteers are ranked by urgency, distance, and capacity so the neediest match happens first.

Deliver it

A volunteer completes pickup

Route and pickup details are shared instantly, and the delivery is logged for full transparency.
